# Security Policy

TRIA SDK v0.1 is pre-production research software. Do not represent it as a certified security, consent, privacy, or safety system.

Please do not publish exploitable vulnerabilities in a public issue. Report them privately to Trivian Institute through its published contact channel and include reproduction steps, affected versions, and potential impact.

The v0.1 kernel prioritizes append-only event semantics, tamper-evident hash chaining, deterministic state reconstruction, explicit governance decisions, scoped consent/revocation, and provenance for epistemic derivation.

Cryptographic signatures, distributed consensus, authentication, authorization infrastructure, encryption-at-rest, and production hardening are not yet provided by Core.
