# Code of Conduct

The TRIA SDK community is committed to a collaborative environment grounded in dignity, consent, non-coercion, intellectual rigor, and good-faith disagreement.

Participants are expected to critique ideas without degrading people; distinguish evidence from interpretation; respect boundaries and privacy; and avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or manipulative conduct.

Project maintainers may moderate contributions or participation that materially undermine these conditions.

For conduct concerns, contact Trivian Institute through its published contact channels.
